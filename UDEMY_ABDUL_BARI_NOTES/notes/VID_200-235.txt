concatenation- just joining two linked lists.
merging- merging all elements from two linked lists.
To check for a LOOP in a linked list use 2 pointers -> move one pointer by 1 step and other by 2 steps.
If they ever meet again then a loop exists else if even one of them becomes NULL then its a linear linked list.

(Singly) Circular Linked List 
Use Head pointer to mark the beginning of a circular linked list.
for a circular linked list with empty node its represented empty node pointing to itself.

Doubly Linked List
Nodes have a pointer to both the next node and the previous node.
Bi-directional traversal is made possible.

Circular DOUBLY Linked List
Gives bi-directional and circular access to its elements.
Insertion anywhere in a Doubly circular LL requires modification of 4 links.

two pointers can be used to find the middle element in a linked list without traversing the linked list more than once.
